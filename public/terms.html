Terms of use

These terms govern use of rco-a2a.ai and its machine surfaces (the kit files, the receipts, the Agent Card, the MCP door) by people and by agents acting for them.

  • 1. What this is. rco-a2a.ai is the Record of Cleared Orders, operated by Agentic KG Holdings, the private office of Matthew Keddy. It holds one receipt per agentic order a gate has cleared and serves each receipt by its decision id. It sells nothing.
  • 2. Receipts. A receipt is what a writer lodged, at the time it lodged it, chained and signed as described on the page. It is not advice and not a representation beyond its own fields. A receipt is never changed or withdrawn; the store is append-only.
  • 3. Writers. write_receipt is open only to writers the office has issued a token to. A writer is responsible for the receipts its token writes.
  • 4. Readers. Anyone may call verify_receipt and read every public path. Agents may not impersonate the door, re-sign its receipts, or present its keys as their own.
  • 5. Keys. Verification is by the published keyring only. Anything not verifiable against it is not the office's.
  • 6. Liability. Receipts are provided as lodged. The office's liability for any use of this surface is nothing: every read is free.
  • 7. Law. British Columbia, Canada.
